Selected work

Agentic UAV swarm coordination

NCRA UAV Dependability Lab, research paper submitted to IEEE TAES (first author)

A three-layer agentic system for UAV swarm task allocation: an algorithmic optimizer, an LLM planning layer and a guardrail layer that enforces safety. I ran ablations across Llama-3, Mistral-7B, Qwen-2.5 and DeepSeek-LLM. Llama-3 performed best by a small margin.

88.2% task completion, 86.7% TCR, zero safety violations across 13 missions on real MAVLink telemetry.
